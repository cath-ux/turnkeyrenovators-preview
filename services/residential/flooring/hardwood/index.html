What the job covers

Wood that suits a humid house

Humidity, slab foundations and seasonal moisture swings all change how wood behaves once it is down. Pick a part of the job to see how we handle it.

Solid hardwood

Milled from one piece of wood, solid boards take many refinishing cycles. They perform best over a wood subfloor in rooms with steady humidity.

  • Many refinishing cycles
  • Needs a wood subfloor
  • Nailed, not floated
  • Higher cupping risk on slabs
Plan My Hardwood

Engineered hardwood

A real wood veneer over a plywood or HDF core resists expansion and contraction, so it is the practical pick for slabs and damp rooms.

  • Glued or floated on slabs
  • Same species and stains
  • Looks nearly identical
  • Refinish count set by veneer

How it works

Six stages, acclimation included

Hardwood punishes shortcuts in this climate, so each stage is part of every job.

  1. 01

    Subfloor check

    We look for soft spots, uneven sections, moisture concerns and height problems, which also decides the right installation method.

  2. 02

    Acclimation

    The boards sit in your home, typically three to seven days, so they adjust to its humidity before they are fastened.

  3. 03

    Installation

    We fasten to the manufacturer's specs, leave the right expansion gaps and plan the layout to minimize cut pieces at thresholds and doorways.

  4. 04

    Stairs and trim

    Treads, nosing, transitions and baseboards are completed with the floor.

  5. 05

    Cleanup

    We haul off every cutoff, box and scrap before leaving.

  6. 06

    Walkthrough

    We walk the finished floor with you before closing out.

Cost

What drives the price of hardwood

Every home is different, so we price after we see the rooms. These five things set your number.

1

Type and species

Solid and engineered sit at different price points, and species, veneer grade and plank size shift cost within each.

2

Room size and layout

More square footage and more corners mean more boards and more hours.

3

Subfloor condition

Leveling, board repairs or moisture remediation come before the wood goes down.

4

Old floor removal

Tearing out and disposing of the current floor adds to the scope.

5

Stair work

Nosing and tread replacement add material and labor beyond the flat floor.

Your written estimate lists each of these, and we go over it line by line before any boards are ordered.

Questions

Hardwood flooring questions

What homeowners ask most about wood floors in this climate.

Can hardwood be installed on a slab foundation?

Engineered hardwood can be glued or floated over a concrete slab with proper moisture mitigation in place. Solid hardwood is not recommended directly on a slab, because it needs a wood subfloor for nailing and is more exposed to moisture coming up from the concrete.

How long does hardwood need to acclimate before installation?

Typically three to seven days in the room where it will be installed, depending on the product and the humidity in your home. That lets the boards adjust before they are fastened down.

What is the difference between solid and engineered hardwood?

Solid hardwood is one piece of wood and supports more refinishing cycles. Engineered hardwood is a real wood veneer over a plywood or HDF core, which keeps it more stable in humid conditions. For slab homes around New Orleans, engineered is generally the better fit.

What species holds up best in high-traffic rooms?

Oak and hickory are among the harder domestic species and do well in hallways, living rooms and dining rooms. Hickory rates harder than oak and resists dents better in very active spaces. Softer species suit lower-traffic rooms like bedrooms.

Can hardwood floors be refinished?

Yes. Solid hardwood can be sanded and refinished many times. Engineered hardwood supports one to two cycles depending on veneer thickness. Either way, the floor has to be sound, with no warping or moisture damage, for refinishing to be worth it.

How long does hardwood flooring installation take?

A single room generally takes one to two days once the boards have acclimated. Multi-room projects typically run three to five days, depending on room count, subfloor condition and any stair work.

Do you install hardwood on stairs?

Yes. Tread and nosing work is included in the project scope where it is needed.

Do you handle removal of existing flooring?

Yes. If the current floor has to come out before the hardwood goes down, we remove and dispose of it as part of the project.
