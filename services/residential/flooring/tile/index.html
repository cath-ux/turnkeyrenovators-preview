What we set

Tile for wet rooms and whole floors

Tile does not warp, swell or soak up humidity, which is why it holds up in South Louisiana. Pick a tile or a room type to see what the job involves.

Porcelain tile

Porcelain is denser and less porous than ceramic, so it resists staining and water better. Large-format porcelain also works through entire first floors.

  • Showers and mudrooms
  • Laundry rooms
  • Large-format living areas
  • Lower water absorption

How it works

Seven stages, prep first

Tile has no flex, so most of the work that decides how long it lasts happens before the first tile is set.

  1. 01

    Subfloor evaluation

    Tile will crack over a subfloor that moves, so we correct soft spots, uneven areas and height issues first.

  2. 02

    Underlayment and waterproofing

    Backer board or membrane goes down where needed, with waterproofing in showers and laundry rooms.

  3. 03

    Layout planning

    We map and center the pattern so cut pieces stay away from thresholds, doorways and focal points.

  4. 04

    Setting and grouting

    Tile is set with proper adhesive coverage and grouted with joints sized for the material and format.

  5. 05

    Transitions and edge trim

    Every room-to-room transition and edge piece is installed as part of the job.

  6. 06

    Site cleanup

    Boxes, broken pieces and debris leave with us.

  7. 07

    Final walkthrough

    You check the finished floor with us before the project closes.

Cost

What sets the cost of a tile floor

Tile pricing depends on the tile and on the prep under it. These five factors go into your estimate.

1

Tile type and format

Porcelain, ceramic and decorative tile differ in price, and large-format, custom or imported tile costs more than standard sizes.

2

Layout complexity

Diagonal patterns, feature strips and intricate cuts take more material and more time than a straight lay.

3

Subfloor condition

Leveling, backer board or moisture remediation add scope before tile goes down.

4

Waterproofing

Showers and laundry rooms need a membrane as an added prep step.

5

Existing floor removal

Pulling up and hauling off the old floor is priced into the job when it is needed.

We walk you through each factor at the estimate, so there are no surprises once the tile starts going down.

Questions

Tile flooring questions

Answers on tile choice, wet areas and timing.

What rooms are best suited for tile flooring?

Tile works anywhere, but it is the standard choice for kitchens, bathrooms, laundry rooms, mudrooms and entryways where moisture resistance and easy cleaning matter. Many homeowners also run large-format tile through first-floor living areas for a consistent look that handles the heat and humidity.

What is the difference between porcelain and ceramic tile?

Porcelain is denser and less porous, so it absorbs less water and performs better in showers and laundry rooms. Ceramic is softer, easier to cut and comes in a wide range of decorative styles, which makes it practical for drier rooms with complex layouts.

Do you handle waterproofing for wet areas?

Yes. Backer board and waterproofing membrane are part of the scope for shower floors, shower walls and laundry rooms where water exposure is ongoing.

What grout type is used for tile installations?

It depends on joint width, tile format and how much moisture the room sees. We recommend the right grout for your project based on the material and how the room is used.

How long does tile flooring installation take?

A single room with a standard layout typically takes one to two days. Complex patterns, large-format tile or wet-area waterproofing generally run two to three days. Multi-room projects are scheduled around total scope and subfloor condition.

Can tile be installed over an existing floor?

In some cases. The existing floor has to be flat, stable, structurally sound and within height limits for doors and transitions. We check it at the estimate and let you know if removal comes first.

Do you remove existing flooring before installing tile?

Yes. When the current floor needs to come out, we remove and dispose of it as part of the project.
