Gate standards

Standards behind a safe gate

Historic guidelines shape how a gate looks, and national safety standards shape how an automated gate must work.

Traditional sizes

Walk gates here are generally three to three and a half feet wide; residential driveway gates are often about ten feet.

Style follows the fence

Gates usually match the fencing, while front entrance gates and their posts are often more ornate than side or rear gates.

Automated gate safety

Gate construction requirements are set out in ASTM F2200, and operators under UL 325 need two separate forms of entrapment protection in each zone.

Control placement

UL 325 requires user controls at least six feet from any moving part of the gate.

Historic district property?

Gates facing the street in historic districts may need design review. We advise on what your property needs during the consultation.

Sources: HDLC Guidelines for Site Elements and DASMA technical guidance on ANSI/CAN/UL 325 and ASTM F2200.

Questions

Gate questions

Answers on matching, permits, automation and upkeep.

Can you match a new gate to my existing iron fence or railing?

Yes. We replicate the bar spacing, decorative patterns and finish so the gate blends in with what is already there.

Can you install a gate if another company built my fence?

Yes. We assess the existing fence and match its style and height so the gate fits naturally into the line.

Do I need a permit for a gate in New Orleans?

It depends on the type of gate, where it sits on the property and your neighborhood. Historic districts may add review requirements, and we tell you what applies during the consultation.

What is the difference between a manual gate and one prepared for automation?

A manual gate uses standard latch and lock hardware and opens by hand. A gate prepared for automation has a reinforced frame and mounting system for a future motor and access controls, so you can upgrade without replacing it.

What safety standards apply to automated gates?

Construction requirements for automated gates are set out in ASTM F2200. Gate operators follow UL 325, which calls for two separate forms of entrapment protection in each entrapment zone.

What maintenance does an iron gate need?

Check it periodically for rust, chipped paint and worn hardware, touch up exposed metal promptly and lubricate the hinges and latch at least once a year.
