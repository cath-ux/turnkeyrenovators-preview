Permits and compliance

What the city and the EPA require first

A demolition is regulated before the first wall comes down. These are the approvals and checks that apply in New Orleans.

Demolition permit

The Department of Safety and Permits needs proof of liability insurance, photos of all four sides of the building and a Letter of Intent or Demolition Request form.

Historic and conservation districts

Properties in the Neighborhood Conservation District or a local historic district need additional approvals before a demolition permit is issued.

Asbestos inspection

Under the EPA asbestos NESHAP, commercial and institutional demolitions need a thorough inspection and advance notice to the delegated agency, often a state office.

Asbestos waste

Asbestos-containing waste must be sealed wet in leak-tight containers, labeled and taken to a landfill qualified to receive it.

Tearing down a house?

The EPA asbestos rule excludes residential buildings with four or fewer units, but not houses torn down as part of a commercial or public project. We confirm which rules apply at the site visit.

Sources: the City of New Orleans guide to building permits and the U.S. EPA overview of the asbestos NESHAP.

A garage and a multi-story building are different jobs in time, equipment and planning.

2

Questions

Demolition questions

Cost, timing, debris and permits, answered plainly.

How much does demolition cost in New Orleans?

It depends on the size and complexity of the project, the materials coming down, site access and disposal fees. We visit the site and give you a quote based on your actual project.

How long does a demolition project take?

It depends on size and complexity. Small residential demolitions may take a few days, while larger commercial or industrial demolitions can take several weeks or months.

Can the demolition be customized to what I need?

Yes. Whether you need a partial demolition, an interior demolition or a complete site clearance, we build the plan around your project.

Do I need a permit to demolish a building in New Orleans?

Yes. The Department of Safety and Permits requires a permit, with proof of liability insurance, photos of all four sides of the building and a Letter of Intent or Demolition Request form. Starting before the permit is issued brings a penalty of at least five times the permit price.

What if my property is in a historic district?

Properties in the Neighborhood Conservation District or a local historic district need additional approvals before demolition. We check your address and build that review into the schedule.

Does a building need an asbestos inspection before demolition?

For commercial, institutional and industrial buildings, yes. The EPA asbestos NESHAP requires a thorough inspection and advance notice before any demolition. Residential buildings with four or fewer units are excluded unless they come down as part of a commercial or public project.

What happens to the debris after demolition?

Debris is sorted and disposed of according to local regulations. Salvageable material may be recycled or repurposed, and non-recyclable material goes out under environmental guidelines.
