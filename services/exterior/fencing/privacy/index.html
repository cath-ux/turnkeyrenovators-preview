Height and placement

How tall, and where it can go

Solid fences are the ones the rules watch most closely. Here is what the city ordinance and historic district guidelines say.

Citywide height

The zoning ordinance limits fences to eight feet, or seven feet inside national historic districts.

Not in the front yard

Front yard fences must be open fences, so solid privacy panels go at the sides and rear.

Side yards in historic districts

Solid wood fencing may not sit closer to the front property line than the main street facade of the house.

Materials in historic districts

In front yards and along streets, HDLC does not permit vinyl, synthetic or lattice fencing, and it prefers solid wood over five feet to stay in rear yards.

Neighbor on the other side?

You generally do not need a neighbor's permission for a fence that sits entirely on your property, but a conversation first helps avoid disputes.

Sources: New Orleans CZO Section 21.6.N and HDLC Guidelines for Site Elements. Rules can vary by neighborhood, and we check yours.

Questions

Privacy fence questions

Answers on height limits, timing and the best material for Louisiana weather.

How tall can a privacy fence be in New Orleans?

Under the Comprehensive Zoning Ordinance, fences may not exceed eight feet, or seven feet in national historic districts. Front yard fences must be open, so solid privacy panels belong at the sides and rear. Historic district rules can add limits, so we check your address first.

How long does privacy fence installation take?

Most residential privacy fences are finished in one to three days, depending on the length of the run and the terrain. Larger projects or lots that need significant ground preparation can take longer.

What is the most durable privacy fence material for Louisiana weather?

Cypress is often considered the top wood for Louisiana because its natural oils repel moisture and insects. Cedar performs well with regular sealing, and pressure-treated pine needs the most upkeep. Composite and vinyl resist humidity with almost no maintenance.

Do I need my neighbor's permission to install a privacy fence?

Not typically, as long as the fence sits entirely within your property lines. Talking with your neighbors before installation still helps keep relationships good and avoid disputes.

Can I install a vinyl privacy fence in a historic district?

Not where it faces the street. For front yards and fences along streets or sidewalks, HDLC guidelines do not permit vinyl or synthetic fencing, so wood with a cap on vertical boards is the usual choice there. We confirm what applies to your lot before design.

Will a privacy fence cut down on noise?

A solid fence helps absorb and block sound from streets, businesses and neighbors, so the yard is quieter for conversation and dining outside.
