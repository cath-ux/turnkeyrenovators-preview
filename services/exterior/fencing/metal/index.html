Historic districts

Metal fence rules in historic districts

Many New Orleans neighborhoods sit inside local historic districts, where the Historic District Landmarks Commission reviews metal fence details before they go in.

Approved metals

For front yards and street-facing lines: wrought or cast iron, or an alternate such as aluminum, typically with a matte black painted finish.

Punched pickets

Pickets must pass through the horizontal rails, not be welded to the face of them.

Front yard height

A metal picket fence in a front yard cannot exceed five feet, chain wall included.

Not permitted

In front yards and along streets, chain-link and hollow tube metal fencing are not permitted, and decorative elements are reviewed before installation.

Building on a chain wall?

Low masonry chain walls under metal fences are limited to 18 inches in these districts, with a brick or stucco finish approved by HDLC staff.

Source: City of New Orleans HDLC Guidelines for Site Elements. We confirm your property's district and rating before design.

Questions

Metal fence questions

Answers on lifespan, rust, storms and historic districts.

How long does metal fencing last in New Orleans?

With proper installation and occasional maintenance, metal fencing typically lasts 20 to 50 years. Aluminum and powder-coated steel tend to last longest because they resist corrosion best.

Does metal fencing rust in a humid climate?

Modern metal fencing is made to resist rust. Aluminum resists corrosion naturally, and steel is protected by galvanizing and powder coating.

Is metal fencing more expensive than wood?

The upfront cost is generally higher than wood. Metal usually delivers better long-term value because it needs less maintenance, fewer repairs and lasts much longer.

Can a metal fence hold up in a hurricane?

A quality metal fence with proper post depth and anchoring handles high wind better than most fencing materials. We design our installations with Louisiana's storm season in mind.

Do you install metal fencing for businesses?

Yes. We install professional-grade metal fencing for retail locations, office complexes, industrial facilities and multi-family housing.

Can I install aluminum fencing in a New Orleans historic district?

HDLC guidelines allow aluminum as an alternate to wrought or cast iron, typically with a matte black finish and pickets punched through the rails. Front yard metal fences are limited to five feet including any chain wall.
