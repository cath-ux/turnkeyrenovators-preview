How it works

From consultation to reopening

Every day a store is closed or blocked costs revenue, so the schedule is built around that from day one.

  1. 01

    Site assessment

    We walk the store with you, document existing conditions and flag structural, electrical or code issues.

  2. 02

    Scope and proposal

    A written proposal covers demolition, construction, materials, timeline and cost, plus a phasing plan if you stay open.

  3. 03

    Permitting

    We submit every required application to the Department of Safety and Permits or the relevant parish office.

  4. 04

    Phased construction

    Open stores get barriers and off-hours noisy work. Closed stores get a compressed schedule to reopen fast.

  5. 05

    Inspection and handoff

    We schedule inspections, finish the punch list and walk the space with you before turnover.

Cost

What drives the cost of a retail remodel

Knowing the variables helps you set a realistic budget before construction starts.

1

Square footage

More area means more material, more labor hours and often a more complex phasing plan.

2

Scope of work

Paint, flooring and lighting cost less than structural changes, panel upgrades or plumbing work.

3

Material selections

Commercial-grade materials rated for retail traffic cost more up front and last longer.

4

Code upgrades

Work that triggers ADA or fire suppression upgrades adds cost, and those upgrades are required by law.

5

Phasing

Staying open means scheduling around business hours with barriers, noise control and safety measures.

You get a detailed, itemized estimate before work begins, so nothing changes on you once construction starts.

Questions

Retail renovation questions

Staying open, timelines, permits and materials, by topic.

Can I keep my retail store open during a renovation?

Yes. We regularly phase retail work so stores stay open, with barriers between construction zones and customer areas and high-impact work scheduled for off-hours or weekends.

How long does a retail store renovation take in New Orleans?

A cosmetic update with paint, flooring and lighting typically takes one to three weeks. A full renovation with layout, electrical and plumbing changes can take four to eight weeks, depending on permitting and the size of the space.

Do I need a permit to renovate a retail store in New Orleans?

Most retail renovations need permits from the City of New Orleans Department of Safety and Permits. Structural, electrical and plumbing work and any change to the occupancy classification all need separate permits, and we handle permitting as part of the scope.

What type of flooring is best for a retail store in New Orleans?

Luxury vinyl plank and porcelain tile are popular because both handle our humidity and are rated for heavy foot traffic. Polished concrete suits stores with an industrial or modern look.

Do you handle storefront and exterior updates?

Yes. The work can include facade updates, signage, windows and doors, awnings and ADA-compliant entrances, coordinated with the interior work to keep the timeline tight.

Can you work with my franchise brand standards?

Yes. We work with franchise operators to meet corporate brand guidelines, including specific materials, color palettes, fixture types and layout requirements.

What is an ADA compliance upgrade in a retail setting?

It typically means aisles at least 36 inches wide, a lowered checkout counter section, an accessible entrance with a ramp or automatic door, and restrooms upgraded to current accessibility standards.

Do you coordinate with landlords on tenant improvement projects?

Yes. We work with landlords, property managers and tenants on tenant improvement projects, helping define a scope, budget and timeline that satisfies everyone on the lease.
