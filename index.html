Need help?

We've got the answers

Straight answers about working with us, from the first consultation to the final walkthrough.

How do I get started with TurnKey Renovators?

Contact us through our website or give us a call. Our team will schedule a consultation to discuss your ideas, budget and timeline.

How long does the renovation process typically take?

The duration depends on the project's scope. We give you a realistic timeline during planning, and we stand behind our promise to start and finish on time.

Do you offer warranties?

Yes. On roofing, we offer a 50-year non pro-rated warranty, a 10-year workmanship warranty and 15-year unlimited wind warranty coverage from named storms. Ask us what applies to your project.

Do you provide post-renovation maintenance services?

Yes. We offer post-renovation maintenance packages to keep your newly transformed space in great shape. Our commitment extends beyond project completion.

Can I choose eco-friendly options for my renovation?

Absolutely. We offer eco-friendly materials and energy-efficient solutions for homeowners who want a greener renovation.

What sets TurnKey Renovators apart?

Our commitment to innovation, quality craftsmanship and client satisfaction. We put your vision first and deliver a personal, exceptional renovation experience.
