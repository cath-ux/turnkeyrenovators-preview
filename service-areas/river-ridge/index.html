What renovating in River Ridge means

River Ridge is not a city, so the parish handles permits. Most of its homes went up in one post-war burst, which tells us a lot before we walk in.

Unincorporated Jefferson Parish

River Ridge is an unincorporated community, so renovation permits come from Jefferson Parish, not a city hall. We prepare and submit the parish permits for your project.

Post-war housing

Large-scale suburban building began here in the early 1950s, and by 1965 nearly all the land between Harahan and Kenner along the river was developed. Many River Ridge homes date from that era.

A river-edge community

River Ridge is bounded by the Mississippi River on its southwest side, across from Waggaman. We plan drainage and moisture protection into our work here.

All About River Ridge, LA

Bordered by Harahan, Kenner and Metairie, River Ridge is an unincorporated Jefferson Parish community on the Mississippi River's east bank. Mostly residential, it runs for roughly three miles beside Jefferson Highway and is recognized for its shady neighborhood streets, local parks, youth baseball and the river levee at its edge.

River Ridge saw large-scale home building start in the early 1950s, leaving a housing stock where many of the homes have stood for several decades and often show an earlier era. That age shapes how we approach kitchens, exteriors, whole-home updates and the local businesses we work on, and we walk through the choices with each owner during the estimate.

Interesting Facts About River Ridge, LA

  • Formerly Little FarmsUntil 1974 the post office knew the area as Little Farms. The name officially changed to River Ridge that year, though Little Farms Avenue keeps the old name alive.
  • Roots in Sugar PlantationsDuring the late 1700s and the early 1800s, French colonists and their descendants established sugar plantations in this area. Sauve Road, which is still a main street, is a reminder of the Sauvé family, who were early landowners.
  • Where the 1849 Flood StartedIn May 1849 the Mississippi broke the levee on the plantation of Pierre Sauvé, on land that is now River Ridge. Known as Sauvé's Crevasse, the break sent floodwater over a large part of New Orleans.
  • World Series ChampsEastbank Little League of River Ridge won the 2019 Little League Baseball World Series with an 8 to 0 victory over Curaçao, the first title ever for a Louisiana team.

Things To Do in River Ridge, LA

  • River Ridge LibraryLocated at 8825 Jefferson Highway, this branch of the Jefferson Parish Library opens six days a week and offers computers, books and free programs for readers young and old.
  • Little Farms PlaygroundA Jefferson Parish recreation site, it offers tennis courts, baseball fields, meeting rooms and tot lots, and it serves as home base for local recreation programs for both youth and adults.
  • Jesse Owens ParkFamilies can spend an easy afternoon outdoors at this neighborhood park and playground, which has open green space beneath large shade trees and a covered pavilion for picnics.
  • Mississippi River Levee TrailLinking the Bonnet Carré Spillway with Audubon Park in New Orleans over 22 miles, the paved path atop the east bank levee goes right past River Ridge and makes a good route for river views and biking.

Notable Residents of River Ridge, LA

  • Jonathan WellsThis former running back starred at John Curtis Christian School in River Ridge, played at Ohio State and was picked by the Houston Texans in the fourth round of the 2002 NFL Draft.
  • Clarence LeBlancA former strong safety from River Ridge, he played at John Curtis Christian School before becoming a three-year starter at LSU.

Public Transportation in River Ridge, LA

Linking Kenner with New Orleans, the E3 Jefferson Highway route of Jefferson Parish Transit passes through River Ridge and stops at Little Farms Avenue. The main road is Jefferson Highway (LA 48), with Airline Drive and Interstate 10 a short drive to the north, while Louis Armstrong New Orleans International Airport close by in Kenner.
