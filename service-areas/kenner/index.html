What renovating in Kenner means

Kenner is its own city with its own permit office and a current building code. Much of it also sits below sea level. We plan every Kenner job around both.

City of Kenner permits

Kenner is an incorporated city, so renovation permits come from its Inspection and Code Enforcement Department on 18th Street, not from Jefferson Parish. We prepare and file them for you.

Built to the 2021 codes

The city currently enforces the 2021 International Building, Residential and Existing Building Codes. Our plans for additions, remodels and repairs are drawn to meet them.

Storm repairs still need a permit

After Hurricane Francine the city waived permit fees for storm damage, but it still requires a permit for repair work. We pull that permit before we start.

Below sea level

Kenner's recorded elevation is about 7 feet below sea level, between Lake Pontchartrain and the Mississippi River, so we pay close attention to moisture, drainage and water-resistant materials.

Sources: City of Kenner Permits and Licensing; Kenner, Louisiana (Wikipedia).

All About Kenner, LA

Running from Lake Pontchartrain down to the Mississippi River, Kenner forms the western edge of Jefferson Parish. Minor Kenner founded it in 1855 on land that had been his family's plantations, and by the 2020 census it ranked as Louisiana's sixth most populous city. Rivertown, the historic district, holds down the riverfront, while Louis Armstrong New Orleans International Airport lies within city limits.

Much of Kenner filled in during the late 1950s and 1960s, when construction of Interstate 10 drove suburban expansion, so we see a lot of homes dating to that period. In houses of that age, our renovation planning often looks at the whole property, from kitchens and living spaces to exteriors, and the same thinking carries over when a local business wants its space refreshed.

Interesting Facts About Kenner, LA

  • Established in 1855In 1855, Minor Kenner laid out the town across three of his family's plantations beside the Mississippi River. Incorporation as a town came in 1867.
  • A Championship Bout by the RiverKenner hosted a bare knuckle heavyweight title fight on May 10, 1870, when Jem Mace defeated Tom Allen. Today the two boxers are remembered with bronze statues at LaSalle's Landing.
  • The Area's Main AirportInside the Kenner city limits, at 1 Terminal Drive, you'll find Louis Armstrong New Orleans International Airport, the primary commercial airport serving the New Orleans region.
  • NASA Prototype on DisplayA full size prototype of NASA's International Space Station is on exhibit at the Rivertown science complex.

Things To Do in Kenner, LA

  • Kenner Planetarium and MegaDomeOn Saturdays the public is welcome at 2020 Fourth Street in Rivertown, home of the Kenner Planetarium and MegaDome Cinema, where shows play beneath a 50 foot dome.
  • Laketown on the LakeWhere Williams Boulevard reaches its north end at Lake Pontchartrain, Laketown offers a fishing pier, a boat launch, pavilions, walking paths and a playground, making it an easy place to pass an afternoon.
  • Heritage Park and LaSalle's LandingHeritage Park in Rivertown recreates a village of historic Kenner buildings in replica form and holds concerts each spring and fall. Afterward, head to LaSalle's Landing near the levee to see the boxer statues.
  • Rivertown Farmers MarketLocal produce and baked goods fill the Rivertown Farmers Market, held at LaSalle's Landing, on Saturdays for most of the year, though the market pauses for summer in August and September.

Notable Residents of Kenner, LA

  • Wynton MarsalisThe trumpeter was born in New Orleans in 1961 and raised in Kenner. In 1997 he became the first jazz musician awarded the Pulitzer Prize for Music.
  • Jon BatisteA Metairie native who spent his childhood in Kenner, the pianist and bandleader took home the 2022 Grammy for Album of the Year and shared the Oscar for Best Original Score for Soul with Trent Reznor and Atticus Ross.

Public Transportation in Kenner, LA

Kenner is served by Jefferson Parish Transit through lines such as the E1 Veterans Airport, E2 Airline Drive and E9 Williams, together with an on demand zone and the E201 Kenner Loop. Interstate 10 runs through the city, and the region's airport, Louis Armstrong New Orleans International Airport, is located right in Kenner.

Do you take on projects in Kenner?

Yes. Kenner is one of our regular service areas, from the older blocks around Rivertown to the neighborhoods north of Interstate 10.

Who issues renovation permits in Kenner?

The City of Kenner Inspection and Code Enforcement Department, at 1926 18th Street. Kenner is incorporated, so its permits come from the city rather than Jefferson Parish. We file the applications for your project.

Do I need a permit for a fence or concrete work in Kenner?

Yes. The city requires a building permit for fences, cement work, pools and prefab buildings, and every demolition must be permitted. We include these permits in our planning.

What services do you offer in Kenner?
