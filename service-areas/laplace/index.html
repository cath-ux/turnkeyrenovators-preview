What renovating in LaPlace means

LaPlace has seen flooding, tornadoes and hurricanes, and it answers to the parish for permits. We build with both in mind.

St. John the Baptist Parish permits

LaPlace is an unincorporated community, so permits come from the parish Permit Division at 1811 W. Airline Hwy in LaPlace. That includes renovations, additions, demolitions and reroofing. We file them for you.

Storm-tested homes

Hurricane Isaac flooded about 5,000 LaPlace houses in 2012, tornadoes hit the town in 2016, and Hurricane Ida caused heavy damage in 2021. Roofing, siding and interior repairs here start with storm resistance.

Levee protection on the way

The state's West Shore Lake Pontchartrain project will build about 18 miles of levees and floodwalls to add protection for LaPlace, Reserve and Garyville.

Sources: St. John the Baptist Parish Building Permits; LaPlace, Louisiana (Wikipedia); Coastal Protection and Restoration Authority, West Shore.

All About LaPlace, LA

About 25 miles west of New Orleans, LaPlace occupies the Mississippi River's east bank in St. John the Baptist Parish. The name comes from Basile Laplace, on whose property the railroad built its 1883 depot. This is where Interstate 55 meets its end at Interstate 10, and across Louisiana the town is famous for its smoked andouille sausage.

When Hurricane Ida struck LaPlace in August 2021, it left many homes needing major repairs to walls, floors and bathrooms. That experience shapes how we approach renovation here, from full interiors and kitchens to exteriors and business spaces, with materials and sealed details chosen to resist water and limit damage if it ever gets inside again.

Interesting Facts About LaPlace, LA

  • A Town Named for a PharmacistIn 1883, New Orleans pharmacist and planter Basile Laplace allowed the railroad to run across his property. His name went first to the depot, next to the post office, and at last to the town itself.
  • Andouille Capital of the WorldDuring the 1970s, Governor Edwin Edwards declared LaPlace the Andouille Capital of the World, a title St. John the Baptist Parish marks every year with its Andouille Festival.
  • Starting Point of the 1811 UprisingLed by Charles Deslondes, the 1811 German Coast Uprising, in which enslaved people revolted and marched toward New Orleans, began at Woodland Plantation in LaPlace.
  • Where I-55 EndsLaPlace marks the southern terminus of Interstate 55, which meets Interstate 10 here before heading north toward Hammond and Jackson, Mississippi. US 51 also comes to an end in town.

Things To Do in LaPlace, LA

  • Cajun Pride Swamp ToursStarting from 110 Frenier Road in LaPlace, guided boat tours wind through the Manchac Swamp, where riders look out for alligators, turtles and cypress trees draped in moss.
  • Andouille FestivalCome to LaPlace in mid October for the Andouille Festival hosted by St. John the Baptist Parish, featuring live music, food vendors, a pageant, a second line parade and a run or walk.
  • Whitney PlantationAcross the river in Wallace, Whitney Plantation is a museum in St. John the Baptist Parish that uses more than a dozen preserved structures to tell the history of slavery.
  • Bonnet Carre SpillwayJust downriver of LaPlace, the Bonnet Carre Spillway, an Army Corps of Engineers floodway between the Mississippi and Lake Pontchartrain, draws people to fish, crawfish, bike and camp.

Notable Residents of LaPlace, LA

  • Kid OryBorn on Christmas Day 1886 in LaPlace and raised on Woodland Plantation, the jazz trombonist and bandleader went on to help pioneer early New Orleans jazz.
  • Russell Batiste Jr.Best known for his years drumming with the funky Meters, the funk drummer made his home in LaPlace and died there in 2023 at age 57.

Public Transportation in LaPlace, LA

Headquartered in LaPlace, the River Parishes Transit Authority offers call ahead service throughout St. Charles and St. John the Baptist Parishes. Interstates 10 and 55, along with US 51 and US 61 (Airline Highway), all serve LaPlace, and the nearest commercial airport is Kenner's Louis Armstrong New Orleans International Airport.
