All about Metairie

All About Metairie, LA

Set in eastern Jefferson Parish, Metairie is an unincorporated community that starts at the New Orleans line along the 17th Street Canal and stretches west toward Kenner, while Lake Pontchartrain forms its northern edge. The community took shape on Metairie Ridge, a natural levee from long ago, and people know it for shopping along Veterans Memorial Boulevard, the lakefront at Bucktown and the spot where the Causeway reaches its southern end.

Housing in Metairie spans everything from Old Metairie, with its 1920s streets, to subdivisions built much more recently, so the homes, kitchens, exteriors and business spaces we work on differ widely in age and layout. An older property can hide work that needs attention behind its finishes, while a newer one may only call for targeted updates, which is why we scope each project before we put together an estimate.

Interesting Facts About Metairie, LA

  • A Name Rooted in French FarmingIn French, métairie means a small tenant farm whose tenant paid the landowner with part of the harvest. French settlers started working the land here in the 1720s, and the name has lasted ever since.
  • Rising From Metairie RidgeMetairie Ridge, the natural levee that Bayou Metairie left behind, is where the community took root. That bayou was an ancient offshoot of the Mississippi River. Today Metairie Road traces the same high ground and remains a main route into New Orleans.
  • Start of the CausewayMetairie is where the Lake Pontchartrain Causeway touches down at its southern end. The bridge runs roughly 24 miles to Mandeville, and Guinness World Records names it the longest continuous bridge over water.
  • Home Base of the SaintsAirline Drive in Metairie is home to the headquarters and practice facility of the New Orleans Saints, and the team's summer training camp has been held there since 2017.

Things To Do in Metairie, LA

  • Lafreniere ParkSet on Downs Boulevard, this parish park covers 155 acres and offers a jogging trail of roughly two miles, a lagoon of 20 acres, picnic shelters, playgrounds, a carousel and a disc golf course with 18 holes.
  • Bucktown Harbor ParkStroll the Bucktown boardwalk beside the lake, take in marsh views from the top of the Bird's Nest Learning Pavilion (shaped like a tree), or put a kayak in the sheltered paddle canal and head out into Lake Pontchartrain.
  • Bonnabel Boat LaunchFound where Bonnabel Boulevard meets the lake, this park offers four boat ramps, a fishing pier, picnic shelters, a dog park and a walking trail that follows the shoreline of Lake Pontchartrain.
  • Lakeside Shopping CenterAlong Veterans Memorial Boulevard, this longtime Metairie indoor mall gathers more than 120 restaurants and stores, making it an easy place to shop or grab lunch when the afternoon turns rainy.

Notable Residents of Metairie, LA

  • Ellen DeGeneresBorn in Metairie in 1958, the comedian later hosted a daytime talk show for nearly two decades.
  • Sal KhanBorn in Metairie in 1976, the man who founded the nonprofit Khan Academy finished at Grace King High School in Metairie as valedictorian.

Public Transportation in Metairie, LA

Several fixed Jefferson Parish Transit routes serve Metairie, among them the E5 Causeway, E1 Veterans and E2 Airline Drive lines, along with an on-demand zone. Most traffic moves on Interstate 10, Airline Drive, Veterans Memorial Boulevard and the Causeway, while Louis Armstrong New Orleans International Airport sits right next door in Kenner.
